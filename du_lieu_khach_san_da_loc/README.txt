BO DU LIEU KHACH SAN DA LOC
Nguon: data.zip va Quy_dinh_du_lieu_va_danh_muc_dropdown_khach_san.docx

Noi dung:
- Moi bang du lieu co mot file CSV va mot file JSON dong bo.
- hotel_rag_knowledge_base.json va hotel_web_dashboard.json duoc giu nguyen cau truc goc vi la tai nguyen RAG/cau hinh, khong phai bang quan he CSV.
- Du lieu quan he duoc loc theo 6 chi nhanh mau dau tien; cac bang phu thuoc duoc giu theo khoa lien ket.
- Loai phong duoc chuan hoa ve 6 ma LP001-LP006: Standard, Superior, Deluxe, Suite, Family, Presidential Suite.
- BANG_GIA duoc rut gon thanh 6 loai phong x (5 mua theo Word + 7 su kien le) = 72 dong.
- Ma mua: M01 Mua thap diem; M02 Mua trung binh; M03 Mua cao diem; M04 Mua le Tet; M05 Mua he.
- 7 su kien le: Tet Duong lich, Tet Nguyen dan, Gio To Hung Vuong, 30/4-1/5, Quoc te Thieu nhi, Quoc khanh, Giang sinh.
- Ngay am lich duoc ghi bang mo ta am lich, can quy doi sang ngay duong theo tung nam khi ap dung.
- Trang thai DAT_PHONG nguon "Da thanh toan" duoc chuyen thanh "Da xac nhan"; "Hoan tat" chuyen thanh "Da tra phong" theo danh muc trong Word.
- Cac dong khong con khoa lien ket hop le bi loai; cac ban ghi trung khoa chinh bi loai.
- Gia ngay le la gia de xuat tinh tu gia tham chieu thap nhat theo loai phong va ty le giam; can duyet truoc khi ap dung kinh doanh.
- Du lieu nguon la du lieu mau nam 2025; khong the xem la du lieu giao dich thuc te da xac minh.
